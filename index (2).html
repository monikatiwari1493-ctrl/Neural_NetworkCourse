Practice task (week 2, CO2)

Take the small dataset provided by your faculty (To be added) and complete the following in your worksheet:

  1. Describe how you would apply cleaning, integration, transformation, reduction and discretization to the data.
  2. Calculate the descriptive statistical measures covered in class.
  3. Write your explanation in Hindi or English, keeping the technical terms in English.
