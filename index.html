Supplied outcomes

Course outcomes CO1 to CO4 from the attached syllabus, quoted as supplied.

  • CO1: To understand the basics of data warehousing and mining.
  • CO2: To learn the data preprocessing, language, architectures, concept description.
  • CO3: To learn the association rules and its algorithms.
  • CO4: To learn the classification and clustering rules and the respective algorithms.

Practice task: bilingual glossary starter

Outcome CO1, Weeks 1 to 3. This builds on the Week 1 glossary-building activity.

  1. Choose five terms from the Weeks 1 to 3 topics, for example data warehouse, multidimensional data model, architecture or implementation.
  2. For each term, write the English technical term, a Tamil explanation in your own words, and one example.
  3. Swap lists with a partner and check that the English terms match the textbook usage.
  4. Keep your list. You will add terms from later weeks.
